# Nehemiah Stem Research — IETF Internet-Draft research portfolio (v1)

These are initial RFCXML v3 individual-submission drafts dated 18 November 2026.

1. `draft-katerega-decentralized-datacentre-ops-00.xml` — decentralised data-centre site operations; intended discussion with CATS, with NMRG/DINRG as related research communities.
2. `draft-katerega-energy-aware-edge-datacentres-00.xml` — energy-aware edge data-centre use cases and evaluation; intended discussion with GREEN.
3. `draft-katerega-mars-surface-computing-continuity-00.xml` — local computing service continuity for Mars surface networks; intended discussion with TIPTOP and DTN.

Important:
- These are research drafts, not IETF-approved documents or working-group-adopted work.
- They deliberately do not reuse or refer to SW103K, Jose, or AfriQloud work.
- They are informational starting points and need technical review, overlap checks, author review, and RFCXML validation against the current IETF tooling/schema before submission.
- The CATS, GREEN, and TIPTOP groups already have related work. The drafts explicitly aim to complement rather than duplicate it; working-group fit and adoption are not guaranteed.
- Author metadata is currently set to Micheal Katerega / Nehemiah Stem Research Ltd. Add a contact email only after confirming the address to publish.
